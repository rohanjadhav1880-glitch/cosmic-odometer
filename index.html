Flight Manual

1. What is "Distance Apart"?
Because the Galaxy is moving at 2.1 million km/h, the Earth was in a completely different part of the universe when your friend was born compared to when you were born.
2. What is "Rotated"?
Distance moved due to Earth's spin. At the equator: ~1,600 km/h (1,000 mph). At poles: 0.
3. What is "Orbited Sun"?
Earth races around the Sun at ~107,000 km/h (66,000 mph). This is your yearly lap.
4. What is "Solar Travel"?
The Sun drags the planets around the Milky Way at ~792,000 km/h (490,000 mph).
5. What is "Galactic Travel"?
Our Galaxy moves at ~600 km/s (simplified) relative to the cosmic background.
